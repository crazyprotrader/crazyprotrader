    FAQ

    What is Crazy Pro Trader?

    A trading education brand founded by Maruf, focused on structured learning, market understanding, risk management and trading psychology.

    Who can join the programs?

    The mentorship is described as Beginner to Advanced. Ask support which program fits your level.

    Do I need previous trading experience?

    Please confirm with support. The mentorship curriculum starts from basic foundations.

    What topics will I learn?

    Price action, candle reaction, market structure, liquidity, SMC and ICT concepts, risk management and trading psychology.

    Are recorded lessons available?

    Yes, the Recorded Trading Class and the mentorship include recorded lessons.

    How does mentorship work?

    It combines live classes, recorded lessons, live market analysis, trade review and personal guidance. See the Mentorship page.

    How can I enroll?

    How can I contact support?

    Is trading risk-free?

    No. Trading involves financial risk and you can lose money. No profit is guaranteed.
